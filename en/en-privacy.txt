PRIVACY POLICY AND PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING NOTICE (GDPR)
Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Trendfarm Labs. The confidentiality and security of our users data are our highest priority. This privacy policy explains transparently what data we collect, how we process it, and how we guarantee full compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Digital Markets Act (DMA).

1. DATA CONTROLLER
The Data Controller for the personal data can be reached for any communication or request to exercise legal rights at the following official email address: info@trendfarm.it.

2. TYPES OF DATA COLLECTED AND PURPOSES
This web portal collects exclusively the data strictly necessary to provide the commercial services requested by the user, categorized into two fields:

A) Data voluntarily provided by the user (Order Forms):
By filling out the license activation form for our software, the user provides: Name and Surname / Corporate Name, Country of residence, Email Address, and the Authorized Broker Account Number.
Purpose: Order management, mandatory legal invoice issuance, generation of the unique Expert Advisor activation key, and technical support communications.

B) Navigation and statistical measurement data (Google Analytics 4):
Subject to explicit consent given via the cookie banner on the Home Page, the website collects aggregated and anonymous data regarding navigation (pages visited, time spent on site, generic geographic location).
Purpose: Website performance optimization and statistical traffic monitoring.

3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
The processing of data for purchasing purposes (A) is based on the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures. The processing of statistical data (B) is based solely on the explicit and free consent granted by the user.

4. DATA RETENTION AND SECURITY MEASURES
Personal and commercial data entered into the forms are not stored or archived in online databases vulnerable to cyberattacks. All information is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption / FormSubmit directly to the Controllers protected Aruba corporate email inbox. Data will be stored only for the duration required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

5. GOOGLE CONSENT MODE V2 COMPLIANCE
This website integrates the latest European security standards. Upon entry, all Google Analytics tracking features are strictly set to "denied" mode. The system will unlock statistical measurement only if the user explicitly clicks the "Accept" button. If rejected, navigation continues anonymously without deploying any profiling cookies.

6. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT
In compliance with Articles 15-22 of the GDPR, users have the right at any time to:
- Request access to their personal data and receive a copy.
- Request the rectification or correction of inaccurate data.
- Request the total and permanent deletion of their data (Right to be forgotten).
- Withdraw previously granted statistical consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply submit a written request without formalities to the email address: info@trendfarm.it. The Data Controller will respond within 24 business hours.
